Privacy

The short version: the apps collect nothing about you. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. What you make stays on your device.

The apps

This covers every Scattered Pearls app, including Blitzfingers. None of them collect, store or send personal data to us or to anyone else. They contain no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and they need no account.

What stays on your device

Everything you create — sounds, recordings, banks, settings, MIDI mappings — is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it. If you use iCloud or device backups, your device may include the app's data in that backup; that is handled by Apple under your own backup settings, not by us.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you record a sound, and only after you allow it. Recordings are saved on your device and never uploaded. You can withdraw access at any time in your device's settings.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect wireless MIDI controllers, so they can play the app. No data is sent anywhere over it.

Files you import or share

When you import an audio file or export a bank, you choose the file or the destination through the system's own file picker or share sheet. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, your Apple ID or any payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They cannot identify you, and you can turn this off at any time under Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements.

Links to this website

Links in the apps, such as the guide, open this website in your browser. What happens then is described below.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and loads nothing from third parties — even the fonts are served from this site itself.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your email address and your message only to answer you. We keep the conversation for as long as it takes to deal with your request and delete it afterwards, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Since the apps collect nothing, there is usually nothing to access — but write to us and we will answer.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live.

Changes

If an app ever starts handling data differently, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
