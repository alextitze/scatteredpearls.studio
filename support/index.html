Blitzfingers

The Blitzfingers guide explains every screen. The questions below are the ones it does not cover.

I don't hear anything

Check the volume and where the sound is going — a connected Bluetooth speaker or headphones take it over. Blitzfingers plays even with the silent switch on; it is an instrument, and an instrument that goes quiet on stage is no use.

Recording doesn't work

The app needs microphone access. If you declined it once, allow it again in your device's Settings ▸ Blitzfingers ▸ Microphone.

My MIDI controller doesn't show up

See MIDI in the guide. A wireless controller has to be paired first; a wired one needs a USB adapter that your device supports.

Purchases

The free trial

Blitzfingers is free to try for 14 days — the whole app. The trial ends by itself and never turns into a charge: you only pay if you buy the full version in the app.

New device, or reinstalled the app

A purchase belongs to your Apple ID, not to the device. Sign in with the same Apple ID and use Restore purchase in the app's settings — you never pay twice.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your sounds and your data

Everything you make stays on your device — there is no account and no cloud copy on our side. That also means we cannot recover it for you: keep your device backed up. See Privacy.
